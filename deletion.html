Account Deletion

Last updated: November 2025

Request Account Deletion

To request the permanent deletion of your Geesly account and all associated data, please send an email to our support team.

Please include the following information in your email to help us process your request:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Your Geesly Public ID
  • The email address used to create your account

Send your request to:

geeslyhelp@gmail.com

What Happens Next

Our team will review your request and contact you to confirm the deletion. Once confirmed, this action is permanent and cannot be reversed.

Important Notice

Your profile, matches, messages, and all other personal data will be permanently erased from our systems in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
