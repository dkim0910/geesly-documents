About Geesly

We built Geesly because we believe finding someone special should feel exciting, fair, and fun — not frustrating.

Our Story

Geesly started with a simple observation: most dating apps feel like a chore. You swipe endlessly, matches pile up but go nowhere, and the whole experience starts to feel meaningless. We wanted to build something different — an app where every interaction carries weight and every match actually means something.

That's why we created the 5-star rating system. Instead of a binary swipe left or right, you rate profiles from 1 to 5 stars. A 4 or a 5 is a like, and a match only happens when two people like each other. This means every match on Geesly represents genuine mutual interest, not just a casual swipe.

We launched in 2025 and have been growing steadily ever since. Our community spans multiple countries, and every day real people are making real connections through Geesly's unique approach to dating.

Our Mission & Values

Quality Over Quantity

We believe one meaningful match is worth more than a hundred empty ones. Our 5-star system ensures that when you match with someone, both of you are genuinely interested. No more wading through matches that never respond.

Safety First

Every photo is automatically screened by AI before it appears on the platform. We use advanced spam detection, 24/7 moderation, and give users powerful reporting and blocking tools. Your safety is never an afterthought.

Real Connections

We focus on connecting you with people nearby — within 300km — so you can actually meet in person. Geesly is about building real relationships, not collecting digital pen pals from the other side of the world.

Fair & Accessible

Geesly is free to use — you can match, chat, and connect without ever paying a cent. Our virtual currency (Corns) can be earned just by using the app. Premium is optional and enhances the experience, but it's never required to find love.

How Our Matching Algorithm Works

Transparency matters to us. Here's exactly how Geesly decides which profiles to show you:

1

Who You Can See

We show people of the gender you're looking for, within 300km of you. Anyone you've blocked — or who blocked you, by account or by phone number — never appears, and neither does anyone you've already rated this month.

2

Closest First

People closer to you always come first, from a few kilometers away out to 300km, so in-person dates are actually possible. At the same distance, Premium members are shown first — and someone who sent you a Skein goes to the very top.

3

Your Preferences

Next come the people who fit what you set: your age range, body type and height, shared interests, lifestyle, personality and education. These decide the order — they don't hide anyone, so you never run out of people just because a filter was too tight.

4

Activity & Similar Ratings

People who've been on Geesly recently rank higher, so the people you see are actually around to reply. So do people whose rating score is close to yours — every like you receive adds a point, and every pass takes one away.

5

Monthly Reset

Every month on the 1st, matches and ratings reset (except active chats). This gives everyone a fresh start and ensures you're always seeing new faces — even if you've been on the app for months.

Understanding the 5-Star Rating System

Our rating system is what makes Geesly unique. Instead of a simple like/pass binary, you express your level of interest on a scale. Here's what each rating means:

1⁠–⁠2
Pass

Not interested. The person won't know you rated them, and they won't appear in your feed again.

3
Maybe

Not sure yet. It counts neither way, and they won't appear in your feed again this month.

4⁠–⁠5
Like — Match Potential

You're interested! The other person sees that someone liked them (who it is, if they're Premium or unlock your profile). When you both like each other — a 4 or a 5 both count — it's an instant match and you'll both be notified.

Skein
The Super Like

Premium members can send a Skein: a like with a short message that puts them at the top of the other person's deck. If that person already liked them, it's an instant match and the message starts the chat.

Every 12 profiles you rate earns you 1 Corn — our virtual currency — regardless of the rating you give. So even passing on someone contributes to your progress.

What Are Corns?

Corns are Geesly's virtual currency. We named them "Corns" because, like kernels of corn, small things can grow into something amazing. Corns are the fuel that powers your Geesly experience beyond the basics.

How to Earn Corns (Free)

  • ● Welcome Corns: Every new account starts with 30 Corns — enough to unlock your first chats or invitations right away.
  • ● Rate profiles: Every 12 profiles you rate earns you 1 Corn automatically. Just browse naturally and your balance grows.
  • ● Watch rewarded ads: Choose to watch a short video ad and earn 1 Corn instantly. Completely optional — you're never forced to watch ads.
  • ● Premium bonus: Subscribers receive bonus Corns as part of their subscription.

How to Spend Corns

  • ● Unlock a chat (50 Corns): When you match with someone, use Corns to unlock the chat and start messaging.
  • ● Bypass hourly limit (20 Corns): On a free account you can rate 6 profiles per hour (Premium has no limit). Spend Corns to unlock 6 more ratings immediately.
  • ● See who liked you (5 Corns): Unlock the full profile of someone who liked you.
  • ● Invite to chat (10 Corns): Send a chat invitation to someone who liked you.
