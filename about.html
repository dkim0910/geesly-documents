About Geesly

We built Geesly because we believe finding someone special should feel exciting, fair, and fun — not frustrating.

Our Story

Geesly started with a simple observation: most dating apps feel like a chore. You swipe endlessly, matches pile up but go nowhere, and the whole experience starts to feel meaningless. We wanted to build something different — an app where every interaction carries weight and every match actually means something.

That's why we created the 5-star rating system. Instead of a binary swipe left or right, you rate profiles from 1 to 5 stars. A match only happens when both people give each other 5 stars — the highest possible rating. This means every match on Geesly represents genuine mutual interest, not just a casual swipe.

We launched in 2025 and have since grown to over 10,000 active users. Our community spans multiple countries, and every day real people are making real connections through Geesly's unique approach to dating.

Our Mission & Values

Quality Over Quantity

We believe one meaningful match is worth more than a hundred empty ones. Our 5-star system ensures that when you match with someone, both of you are genuinely interested. No more wading through matches that never respond.

Safety First

Every photo is automatically screened by AI before it appears on the platform. We use advanced spam detection, 24/7 moderation, and give users powerful reporting and blocking tools. Your safety is never an afterthought.

Real Connections

We focus on connecting you with people nearby — within 300km — so you can actually meet in person. Geesly is about building real relationships, not collecting digital pen pals from the other side of the world.

Fair & Accessible

Geesly is free to use — you can match, chat, and connect without ever paying a cent. Our virtual currency (Corns) can be earned just by using the app. Premium is optional and enhances the experience, but it's never required to find love.

How Our Matching Algorithm Works

Transparency matters to us. Here's exactly how Geesly decides which profiles to show you:

1

Preference Filtering

First, we filter based on the preferences you set: gender, age range, distance (up to 300km), and body type. You'll only see profiles that match what you're looking for.

2

Location Priority

People closer to you appear first. We use geolocation to prioritize showing you matches within a realistic meeting distance, making in-person dates actually possible.

3

Rating Score

Users who receive higher average ratings from others are shown more prominently. This creates a natural incentive to put effort into your profile — good photos, thoughtful bios, and genuine information all help you get noticed.

4

Activity & Freshness

Active users get priority. If someone hasn't been on Geesly recently, they'll appear lower in your feed. This means the people you see are actually online and ready to connect.

5

Monthly Reset

Every month on the 1st, matches and ratings reset (except active chats). This gives everyone a fresh start and ensures you're always seeing new faces — even if you've been on the app for months.

Understanding the 5-Star Rating System

Our rating system is what makes Geesly unique. Instead of a simple like/pass binary, you express your level of interest on a scale. Here's what each rating means:

1-3
Pass

Not interested right now. The person won't know you rated them, and they won't appear in your feed again this month.

4
Like

You're interested! This counts as a "like" and the other person will see that someone liked them (but not who, unless they're premium).

5
Super Like — Match Potential

This is the big one. When you give someone 5 stars AND they give you 5 stars back, it's an instant match. You'll both be notified and can start chatting immediately.

Every 12 profiles you rate earns you 1 Corn — our virtual currency — regardless of the rating you give. So even passing on someone contributes to your progress.

What Are Corns?

Corns are Geesly's virtual currency. We named them "Corns" because, like kernels of corn, small things can grow into something amazing. Corns are the fuel that powers your Geesly experience beyond the basics.

How to Earn Corns (Free)

  • ● Rate profiles: Every 12 profiles you rate earns you 1 Corn automatically. Just browse naturally and your balance grows.
  • ● Watch rewarded ads: Choose to watch a short video ad and earn 1 Corn instantly. Completely optional — you're never forced to watch ads.
  • ● Premium bonus: Subscribers receive bonus Corns as part of their subscription.

How to Spend Corns

  • ● Unlock a chat (20 Corns): When you match with someone, use Corns to unlock the chat and start messaging.
  • ● Bypass hourly limit (20 Corns): Normally you can rate 6 profiles per hour. Spend Corns to unlock 6 more ratings immediately.
  • ● View full profile (5 Corns): See all of someone's photos and detailed information before deciding on a rating.
