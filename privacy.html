Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Geesly collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our dating application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information generated when you use the app, and information from third parties.

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: Your email address and sign-in details from Google or Apple Sign-In
  • Profile Information: Name, public ID (username), age, date of birth, gender, photos (2-6 images), bio, interests, personality traits, MBTI type, and languages spoken
  • Demographics & Lifestyle: Job title, company, school, height, body type, education level, relationship intent, workout frequency, smoking and drinking habits, dietary preferences, religious beliefs, political views, zodiac sign, and race/ethnicity. Religious beliefs, political views, race/ethnicity and the gender you're interested in are sensitive details: they are optional, shown on your profile only if you add them, and you can remove them at any time
  • Preferences: Gender preference, age range, height range, preferred body styles, and other matching criteria
  • User Content: Messages in chats (subject to spam detection filters) and Skein messages (short introduction messages, up to 140 characters, sent together with a "Skein" super-like)
  • Phone Verification (optional): If you verify your phone number, we store the number, its country code and when it was verified. The verification code is sent by text message through Firebase Authentication (Google)
  • Blocked Numbers: Phone numbers you choose to enter in the optional "Block by phone number" feature. These are stored in standardized (E.164) format with your account and used solely to hide you and the blocked person from each other's discovery feeds. Blocking by number only takes effect against users who have verified that phone number in the app. The people you block are not notified. The numbers stay on your account until you remove them or delete your account

B. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data: Activity within the app including likes (4–5 stars), passes (1–3 stars), matches, chat invitations sent/received, profiles viewed, rating progress (earn 1 Corn per 12 ratings), hourly rating limits (6 per hour), features used, and login times
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, IP address, device identifiers, mobile network information, and app version
  • Location Data: Precise geolocation (latitude/longitude) and geohash (encoded location) to suggest nearby matches, within 300km (approximately 186 miles), closest first. People farther away are not shown. Users without location data may still be shown, after everyone nearby. Other users are shown your city and approximate distance, not your coordinates; turning off "Share location" in Settings → Privacy hides your city and distance from them. To show a city name, coordinates are looked up with your device's built-in location service (Apple or Google). You can revoke the location permission in device settings, though it will severely limit your visibility to other users
  • Analytics: App performance data, crash reports, and usage patterns via Firebase Analytics (which includes Google Analytics) and Crashlytics (see Section 12)
  • Notification Token: A push-notification token for your device, so we can send you notifications

C. Information from Third Parties

  • Purchase Details: Transaction confirmations from RevenueCat and app stores (Google Play or Apple App Store) for premium subscriptions and Corn purchases. We do not store your payment information directly
  • Authentication: Basic profile information from Google or Apple when you sign in through their services

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: Create your account, display your profile to other users, and facilitate messaging
  • Matchmaking: Show you people of the gender you're looking for within 300km, closest first. Anyone who sent you a Skein comes first; at the same distance, Premium members come next, then the profiles that best fit your preferences (age range, body type, height, shared interests and more), people who were active recently, and people whose rating score is similar to yours. Preferences change the order; they don't hide anyone
  • Safety & Moderation:
    • Check each message as it is sent for links, phone numbers and repeated spam
    • Block messages containing URLs, phone numbers, or repeated spam (5+ identical messages in your last 10)
    • Review user reports for harassment, inappropriate content, spam, and fake profiles, including the reported conversation
    • Automatically suspend accounts reported for spam, or as a fake profile, by 50 or more different users
    • Send report details to our team at hello+geeslyhelp@nelera.net (a report made from a chat includes the last 20 messages)
    • Enforce community standards and Terms of Service
  • Transactions: Process premium subscriptions and Corn purchases via app stores, manage virtual currency balances, and handle restore purchases
  • Gamification: Track rating progress, award Corns for user engagement (1 Corn per 12 ratings), manage hourly rating limits (6 profiles per hour), and unlock rate limit with 20 Corns
  • Communications: Send push notifications and in-app alerts about matches, likes, Skeins, chat invitations, new messages, and when you can rate again. We use email (Gmail) only to send user reports and service statistics to our own team
  • Analytics: Track usage trends via Firebase Analytics to improve features, fix bugs, and optimize performance
  • Advertising: Offer optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob; watching one earns 1 Corn (see Section 12)

4. Spam Detection and Message Filtering

Automatic Message Blocking: To protect users from spam and unwanted contact sharing, we automatically block messages that contain:

  • URLs and Links: Any web addresses, domain names, or links (http://, https://, www., .com, .net, etc.)
  • Phone Numbers: All common phone number formats including international numbers
  • Repeated Spam: Sending the same message 5+ times in your last 10 messages

These filters run in real-time before messages are sent. You'll receive a notification if your message is blocked.

5. Image Scanning and Content Moderation

To maintain a safe and appropriate environment, all profile images uploaded to Geesly are automatically scanned using Google Cloud Vision API before being accepted.

A. What We Scan For

Our automated image analysis checks for:

  • Explicit Adult Content: Full nudity or sexually explicit material (bikini and swimwear are permitted)
  • Violent Content: Images containing violence or weapons
  • Minor Detection: Images that may contain or depict minors (anyone under 18)
  • Spam/Promotional Content: QR codes, advertisements, promotional material, or commercial content
  • Stock Photos: Professional stock or watermark images that are not of you

B. How It Works

When you upload a profile image:

  • The image is sent to Google Cloud Vision API for analysis
  • The API returns safety scores and content labels
  • Images that violate our policies are automatically rejected
  • You receive immediate feedback if an image is rejected, with the reason
  • Approved images are then uploaded to your profile
  • New and changed profile photos also enter a moderation queue and may additionally be reviewed by our moderation team

C. Data Handling

  • Images are analyzed in real-time during upload
  • Google Cloud Vision API processes images temporarily for analysis only
  • Under Google's Cloud Vision data-use terms, images sent for analysis are not stored by Google after the analysis
  • Only approved images are stored on our servers. Profile photos are served from web links that are not password-protected, so anyone who has a photo's link can open it
  • We do not use image data for training AI models

D. Why We Do This

Image scanning helps us:

  • Protect minors by preventing images of children from being uploaded
  • Maintain community standards by blocking explicit or violent content
  • Prevent spam and commercial exploitation of the platform
  • Create a safer dating environment for all users

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your profile information via app settings at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account in the app via Settings → Support → Delete Account. Your profile, photos, messages, matches and other account data are permanently deleted by our server-side deletion process; the records listed under "Kept after deletion" in Section 7 are kept. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in the App Store or Google Play (typically within seconds)
  • Device Permissions: Revoke permissions for Location, Photos, or Notifications through your device settings at any time
  • Notification Preferences: Manage push notifications, alerts, vibration and marketing emails in Settings → Notifications
  • Privacy Settings: In Settings → Privacy you can hide your online status, turn on Incognito mode (you are hidden from discovery, and your likes don't notify anyone or create matches), and stop sharing your location
  • Blocking: Block users to prevent them from seeing your profile or contacting you, or block people you know by their phone number
  • Suspended Accounts: If your account is suspended you can't sign in, so email us to appeal or to have your data deleted

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as follows:

  • Active Accounts: Data is kept while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon account deletion (via in-app delete function), your data is immediately and permanently deleted by our server-side deletion process, including:
    • User profile and authentication data
    • All profile photos from Firebase Storage
    • Chat invitations (sent and received)
    • Chat messages and conversations
    • Chat unlock records
    • Skein records, sent and received (including their messages)
    • References in other users' matches, likes and blocked lists
    • Local app cache and SharedPreferences/UserDefaults
  • Automatic Cleanup:
    • Notifications: Auto-deleted after 7 days
    • Chat Invitations: Answered invitations are deleted after 7 days; pending invitations stay until they are answered
    • Matches & Likes: Reset monthly (1st of each month), except matches with an active chat, blocked users, pending invitations and pending Skeins
    • Rating Progress: Tracked for Corn rewards, resets after earning
  • Kept after deletion: Some records are kept, including after you delete your account:
    • Reports and the conversation included with them, for as long as needed for safety investigations and legal purposes
    • Records of purchases and rewarded-ad credits, for accounting and fraud prevention (Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records too)
    • For accounts suspended or removed by our team, the email address, to prevent abuse
  • Suspended Accounts: Accounts suspended for spam or fake profiles stay suspended. Contact us if you believe this is a mistake

8. Security

We use Firebase's industry-standard security measures, including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Secure cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase with App Check)
  • Firebase Authentication for secure login
  • Rate limiting in Cloud Functions (max 10 notifications per minute, max 3 reports per minute)
  • Spam detection filters to prevent malicious content
  • Automatic suspension of accounts that many different users report for spam or as fake

However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Additionally, no images of minors (anyone under 18 years of age) are allowed on the platform. Users must not upload, share, or post any images containing or depicting minors in any form. Purchasing Corns:
