Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Welcome to Geesly. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Geesly ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use our dating application and related services (the "Service").

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect three categories of information: information you provide, information generated when you use the app, and information from third parties.

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: Phone number, email address, and login credentials.
  • Profile Information: Name, public ID, age, date of birth, gender, photos, bio, prompts, interests, personality traits, and languages spoken.
  • Demographics & Lifestyle: Job title, company, school, height, education, relationship intent, lifestyle choices (e.g., smoking, drinking), religious beliefs, and political views.
  • User Content: Messages, photos, and videos you send to other users.

B. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data: Activity within the app (likes, passes, matches), date/time of login, and features used.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, IP address, unique device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), and mobile network information.
  • Location Data: Precise geolocation (latitude/longitude) to suggest nearby matches. You can revoke this permission in your device settings, though it will limit visibility.

C. Information from Third Parties

  • Purchase Details: If you make a purchase (subscriptions or consumables), we receive confirmation from the app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). We do not process or store your credit card information directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: Create your account, display your profile, and facilitate messaging.
  • Matchmaking Algorithm: We use automated processing to analyze your profile and preferences to suggest the most compatible matches.
  • Transactions: Process subscriptions and one-time purchases via App Stores.
  • Safety & Moderation: Detect and prevent fraud, spam, harassment, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Communications: Send functional notifications (new matches, messages) and marketing updates (which you can opt-out of).
  • Analytics: Analyze usage trends to fix bugs and improve the app experience.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only as follows:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile data is visible to other users to facilitate dating.
  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf, including cloud hosting (e.g., Google Firebase), analytics, and customer support tools.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a court order or subpoena.
  • Vital Interests: We may disclose information if we believe it is necessary to protect the safety of any person.
  • Business Transfers: If Geesly is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your profile via the app settings.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account directly within the app settings. Your data will be removed from our active systems, subject to our retention policy.
  • Device Permissions: You can revoke permissions for Location, Notifications, or Photos through your device settings.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes and as permitted by applicable law.

  • Active Accounts: We keep your data while your account is open.
  • Deleted Accounts: If you close your account, we retain certain data for a safety retention window (typically 3 months) to deter abuse and comply with legal obligations. After this period, data is deleted or anonymized.

6. Security

We utilize industry-standard security measures, including encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Geesly is strictly for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will immediately ban the account and delete the associated data.

8. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred across borders. We take steps to ensure your data is treated securely and in accordance with this policy.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use of Geesly after updates constitutes acceptance.
