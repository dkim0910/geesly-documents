Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Geesly free to use?

Yes! Geesly is 100% free to download and use. You can create a profile, rate other users, match, and chat for free. We also offer premium subscriptions with additional features like unlimited chat unlocks and bonus Corns.

How does the 5-star rating system work?

When you browse profiles, you rate them from 1-5 stars. Giving someone a 4-star rating is a "like." However, when you rate someone 5 stars AND they rate you 5 stars back, it's an automatic match and you can start having conversations! Users with higher ratings get shown more often, creating a quality-driven matching experience.

How do I get a match?

Getting a match is simple! Browse profiles and rate someone 5 stars if you're interested. If they also rate you 5 stars, you'll automatically match and unlock the ability to chat. Both users must give each other 5 stars for a match to happen. Once matched, you can start messaging right away!

How do I report someone?

If you encounter inappropriate behavior, spam, or anything that violates our community guidelines, tap the flag icon on their profile or in the chat. This will report them to our moderation team for review. We take reports seriously and investigate all flagged accounts to keep Geesly safe for everyone.

What are Corns and how do I earn them?

Corns are our virtual currency! Earn 1 Corn for every 12 profiles you rate, or watch rewarded ads to earn more. Use Corns to unlock chats (20 Corns), bypass rating limits (20 Corns), or view full profiles (5 Corns). You can also purchase Corns via in-app purchases.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely! We use Firebase's industry-standard security, monitor for spam and harassment, and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA. Your data is encrypted, and when you delete your account, everything is permanently removed within 45 seconds.

What's the maximum distance for matches?

Geesly shows you potential matches within 300km (approximately 186 miles) of your location. This helps you connect with people you could actually meet in person. Users beyond this distance won't appear in your feed.

Does Geesly conduct background checks?

No, Geesly does not conduct criminal background checks on users. Age verification is self-reported and not independently verified. You are responsible for your own safety when interacting with other users. We recommend meeting in public places, telling friends/family about your plans, and trusting your instincts. Report any suspicious behavior immediately using the flag icon.

Do matches and ratings reset?

Yes! Matches, likes, and your "seen users" list reset automatically on the 1st of every month. This gives you fresh opportunities to discover new people. However, active chats, blocked users, and pending invitations are not affected by the monthly reset and will remain.

What does premium subscription include?

Premium subscribers get unlimited chat unlocks (no 20 Corn cost), unlimited profile views (no 5 Corn cost), unlimited rating with no hourly limits, unlimited chat invitations, bonus Corns included, and priority matching visibility. Upgrade to premium for the full Geesly experience!
